# Viral hook library

100 opening-line skeletons from reels with millions of views (source: Brandit Content swipe file, Oct 2026). Number = rank by views; `M`/`K` = views. Skeletons are shortened; `[…]` is the blank you fill.

## How to use
1. Know what the video says first. Then pick the family that opens it best (table below).
2. Keep the skeleton's rhythm and swap only the blanks: numbers, niche, result.
3. Pick 3 candidates for the brief, show Adham, let him choose. Film or cut the same video with 3 hooks and post them as trial reels. Keep the winner.
4. The first 3 seconds of picture matter as much as the words: show the result, the problem or the face at once.
5. Arabic reels: re-write in Egyptian Arabic with the same rhythm and meaning. Never translate word for word. Keep numbers concrete and short.

## Which family for which video
| Video is about | Families |
|---|---|
| A result we got, a client win | Proof, Time-boxed plan |
| A how-to or tutorial | Fix/problem, Authority-flip, Quick method |
| A hot take | Contrarian, Authority-flip |
| Something we tried or built | Challenge, Tool/AI demo |
| A tips or tools roundup | List |
| Something surprising | Curiosity |
| Founder story, personal | Identity |
